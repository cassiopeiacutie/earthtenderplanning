Hi, I'm Hannah.

Earthward was founded by me, Hannah June Althea. I'm an artist, writer, builder, and land use planner. I care deeply about climate resilience and about ecological, social, and personal growth.

I left conventional planning practice to start a niche firm for landowners interested in sustainable architecture and permaculture design, including cob and straw bale construction, alternative ways of living, intentional communities, and placemaking. Earthward is a bridge between your climate and personal resilience goals and the thorny red tape of the land use approval process.

Rooted in experience

With a background in environmental planning methods, I've learned to plan for the land by living in relation to it, in shared homesteads and ecovillages where every choice about water, shelter, and waste was intentional.

Rooted in place

Each piece of land has its own gifts and challenges, and so does the zoning law that comes with it. I'm passionate about moving through the paperwork and code in a way that helps realize your dream, at less cost than the typical land use consultant.

Rooted in values

I believe a home should ask less of the earth and support the harmony of your life. I'm excited to help you build smaller, simpler, and with what's close at hand. I'll show you what the zoning allows, and keep my costs low so you can put more resources into earth-friendly materials and design.

If you're dreaming of a cob cottage, a straw bale home, an ADU, or a small homestead, I can help you find out what's possible on your land, and how to get there without losing money or heart along the way.
