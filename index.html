When to hire a planner

Earlier than you'd think! A few hours of research before you buy land or draw plans can save you thousands of dollars and wasted time.

  • You're thinking about buying land.
  • You have a building idea that isn't standard.
  • You're not sure what your zoning allows.
  • A city or county gave you confusing answers.
