BEFORE YOU BREAK GROUND

A LITTLE FIELD GUIDE · 02

So, when do you need a land use consultant?

Honestly? Probably sooner than you think.

Before purchasing property, hiring an architect, submitting plans, or investing in construction, it's worth understanding what your land actually allows.

  • You're thinking about buying rural property.
  • You have an unconventional building idea.
  • You want to build a cob house or alternative dwelling.
  • You're unsure what your zoning allows.
  • You've received confusing information from a city or county.
  • You want to avoid spending thousands on plans before understanding whether your project is feasible.
  • You need help figuring out the approval process.

A little homework upfront can save a whole lot of headaches later.
