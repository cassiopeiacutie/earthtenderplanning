When should you call a land use planner?

Earlier than you'd think. Before you buy land, hire a designer, draw up plans, and definitely before you pour a foundation, it's worth knowing what the land allows.

A few hours of research now can save you thousands of dollars and a lot of heartbreak.

Let's talk through your idea
  • You're thinking about buying rural property.

A little about me

My name is Hannah June Althea

I'm an artist, writer, builder, and land use planner.

I left conventional planning because I struggled to keep working on projects that only wanted to be cheap and standard, often at the cost of greenspace. Our relationship with the land we live on is essential to our wellbeing.

I've lived in apartment complexes, shared urban permaculture homesteads, and ecovillages, all of which taught me how beautiful, human-scale, and healing it can be to live close to the land, and how hard it is to get there if you don't already own property.

I believe living on the land should be a reciprocal relationship. When we build with care, we can make something that nourishes our families, our communities, and the land itself.

Planning, while bureaucratic in practice, is deeply entwined with our built environments. Let me help you build something beautiful.
